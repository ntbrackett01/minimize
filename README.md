# Minimize

A Mac utility. Click an app's Dock icon to hide it, click again to bring it back.

## Get it

[Download Minimize](https://github.com/ntbrackett01/minimize/releases/latest/download/Minimize.dmg)

Open the disk image and drag Minimize to Applications. It needs macOS 27 or later.

## What it does

- Click the Dock icon of the app in front, and the app hides, the same as Cmd+H.
- Click the icon of a hidden app, and it comes back.
- Click any other app's icon, and it comes to the front, as always.

Everything else on the Dock works as it does without Minimize.

## The Accessibility permission

Minimize needs the Accessibility permission to see a click on the Dock. The first time you open it, a small window says why and opens System Settings at the right page.

## The menu bar

Minimize lives in the menu bar. Its menu has an on/off switch, Launch at Login, Check for Updates, and Quit.

## Updates

Minimize looks for a newer version once a day. It never installs one without a yes.
